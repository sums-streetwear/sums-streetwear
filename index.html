SUMS is a streetwear brand rooted in Port St Johns, Eastern Cape. Built around movement, individuality and a different way of seeing everyday style, SUMS brings a fresh energy to streetwear.


From the coastline of the Eastern Cape to wherever the next generation takes it, SUMS represents confidence, creativity and the freedom to move differently.
