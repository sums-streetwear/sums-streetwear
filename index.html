ABOUT SUMS

SUMS started with a simple idea: streetwear that carries where you're from, not just what's trending. The name and the story trace back to the Wild Coast — its rawness, its rhythm, its refusal to be anything but itself.

Every piece is built for people building their own path: creators, hustlers, students, dreamers. Not following a legacy — writing one. That's the standard behind every drop, every stitch, every colour we choose.

This is still day one. More pieces, more drops, and more of the SUMS story are coming — follow along on Instagram and TikTok to catch it as it happens.
