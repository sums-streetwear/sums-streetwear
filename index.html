ABOUT SUMS

ROOTED IN
THE EASTERN CAPE.

SUMS is a streetwear brand from Port St Johns, Eastern Cape — built around individuality, confidence and a different way of seeing everyday style.

From the coastal energy of Port St Johns comes a brand with its own direction. SUMS blends clean design with modern streetwear, creating pieces that feel simple, bold and effortless.

SUMS is about more than putting on a shirt or a hoodie. It is about the attitude that comes with it. The confidence to stand out, the freedom to create your own lane, and the mindset to keep moving different.

Born in the Eastern Cape. Made for everywhere.
