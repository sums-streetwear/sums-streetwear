ABOUT SUMS.

SUMS is a premium streetwear brand from Port St Johns in the Eastern Cape, South Africa.

Built around confidence, creativity and the freedom to move differently, SUMS brings a fresh streetwear identity from a place with its own character and energy.

From everyday essentials to pieces that stand apart, SUMS is about making clothing feel personal. Clean designs, strong energy and a mindset that says you don't have to follow the crowd.
